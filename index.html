WELCOME TO DON BOSCO CATHOLIC SCHOOL

Don Bosco Catholic School Dilla is a Catholic co-educational institution located in Dilla, Ethiopia. The school is under the administration of the Salesians of Don Bosco, a religious congregation of the Catholic Church. The secondary school is part of the institution which include kindergarten and primary schools.

ABOUT US

CELEBRATING OUR 25 YEARS ANNIVERSARY

Don Bosco Catholic School in Dilla has been providing quality education for the past 25 years. The school was founded in 1991 E.C and has since then been committed to providing holistic education to its students. Over the years, the school has expanded its facilities and programs to meet the changing needs of the students and the community.

History of our school

Don Bosco Catholic Secondary School started 9th grade with 19 female and 27 male students with a total of 46 students in 1991 with the approval of the Minister of Education. At the time, the school was the only non-governmental secondary school in Gedo Zone.

Biography of saint Don Bosco

John Bosco was born on August 16, 1815, in a small village called Becchi, near Turin, Italy. He was the youngest child of a poor farmer named Francesco Bosco and his wife, Margherita. John's father died when he was just two years old, leaving his mother to raise him and his two older brothers on her own.

St. John Bosco
