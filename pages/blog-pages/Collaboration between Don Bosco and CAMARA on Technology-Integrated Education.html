Collaboration between Don Bosco and CAMARA on Technology-Integrated Education

Camara Education, an award-winning worldwide charitable organisation started in 2005 in Dublin, Ireland, has provided computers for Don Bosco Dilla's three computer laboratories in recent years.

Camara has once again constructed another E-learning lab in the high school department with a. fully. internet-connected. network this year. This is an extra component to our school's strong and quality education, where self-learning for both teachers and students provides them with 21st-century abilities.

Don Bosco Secondary School, which has a strong relationship with Camara and a good ICT curriculum, hosted a conference for primary school teachers from June 5-9, 2023. The workshop was attended by 52 teachers from several schools in the southern region, all of which had acquired computers from Camara Education.

The participants were taught how to use the e-learning facility they were given and how to train others in their schools. These e-learning machines cover the Ministry of Education's curriculum, Don Bosco Dilla has been working with Camara to empower young people via creative education that is reinforced with technology.
