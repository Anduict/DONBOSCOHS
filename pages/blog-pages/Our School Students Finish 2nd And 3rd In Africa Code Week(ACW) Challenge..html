Our School Students Finish 2nd And 3rd In Africa Code Week(ACW) Challenge.

The Africa Code Week Challenge is a coding contest that spans across Africa, inviting all-young individuals from different walks of life from 8 - 16 year to develop a game using the Scratch programming language.

The AfriCAN Code Challenge (ACC), a part of Africa Code Week, has increased in popularity for the third year running. Liya Ayalew and her team (Bethelehem Ayalew and Ihsan Shafi) won the ACC 2022 competition this year, representing the Addis Global Academy in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Melkamu Mezgabu, a fellow student at Dilla Don Bosco High School, came in second, and Melkam Asrat, who took third, was a fellow student there as well. We are also pleased to announce that the work of Liya Ayalew and the team has been submitted globally and won in 6th place. We appreciate how well the winners represented their nation and school. Congrats to the winners!

Our school Grade 9 students melakmu mezgebu and melkam asrat took 2nd and 3rd place in ACW coding comptition. The two students have taken the coding for kid program.

Mr. Mikias Woldeyesus, the general manager of Camara Education in Ethiopia, came to our school and gave prizes to the winners.
For the winners the rewards was:

  • Modern smart phone
  • T-shirt
  • Certificate and Flash disk
