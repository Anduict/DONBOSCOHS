The Quiz Competition Between Grade 9 Students Has Ended

The quiz competition which was held for the last two months among 12 students of Grade 9 selected from each section has ended.

At Dilla Don Bosco Catholic 2nd Secondary School, the 9th grade students were divided into two groups and a highly competitive question and answer competition ended on April 11/2015 in the presence of the school management, teachers and students.

  • Opening speech door/teacher brother asmaru.
  • 4 students who have reached the final were asked two questions from 6 subjects.
  • The student who won 1st place by answering all the questions was the student who previously represented our school and won 2nd place in the Ethiopian Coding Challenge competition.
  • 2nd place - Yibeltal Birhanu
  • 3rd place - Kidist Werkayehu
  • 4th place - Koket Girma
  • Finally, Teacher Anteneh Girma awarded books and certificates to the competing students according to their level.
  • Teachers who participated in the competition were presented with certificates prepared by the school through Tr. Mesay

Such activities should be strengthened to improve students' knowledge and results. The organizing committee would like to express its sincere thanks to those who participated and supported the competition.
The school's Quiz competition committee
