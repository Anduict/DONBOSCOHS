Green Club Background

Dilla Donbosco Green Club is a school based Eco club, which is located in the southern nation Gedeo zone Dilla Woreda, In Donbosco Chatolic school. Donbosco Dilla has two campuses. The main campus of Don Bosco Dilla is situated at the entrance point of the town, 365 km. away from Addis Ababa. DB Dilla extends its operation at Wallame which is 3 Km. away from main campus. The two campuses are in Gedeo zone. The Salesians of Don Bosco started operating the school since 1982. The green club at the two Campuses of Don Bosco Dilla and Wallame was organized in April 21, 2018 through the “13 months of Solar Energy” project.

Donbosco Green Club empowers students to participate and take up meaningful environmental activities and projects. It is a forum through which students can reach out to influence, engage their parents and neighbourhood communities to promote sound environmental behavior. It will empower students to explore environmental concepts and actions beyond the confines of a syllabus or curriculum. While everyone, everywhere, asserts the importance of ‘learning to live sustainably,’ environment remains a peripheral issue in the formal schooling system.
