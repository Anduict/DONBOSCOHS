HISTORY

How it all started

Don Bosco Catholic Secondary School started 9th grade with 19 female and 27 male students with a total of 46 students in 1991 with the approval of the Minister of Education. At the time, the school was the only non-governmental secondary school in Gedo Zone.

In 1992, the school accepted 46 students and taught them in the 9th grade, and these students were the first 10th graders to take the general high school final exam under the new education policy. In 1993, the 46 students who sat for the national exam were able to pass to the preparatory school with a passing score. But since the school has not yet started offering preparatory school they were transfered to other schools.

In 2001/02, former policy students took the 12th grade national exam and most of the students entered various higher education institutions and pursued their studies.

In 2003 the school launch the new policy 11th grade for the first time and accept around 50 students. on the next year those students took the national enterance exam and most of them passed the exam to enter higher education institutions.
