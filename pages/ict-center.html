ICT CENTER HISTORY

ICT Center started its work in 1998 with 10 computers and 1 teacher. In 1999, the center was able to acquire 19 more computers through the great efforts of the then school principal brother Yergalem W/Yohannes. At that time, by the curriculum the subject was given to 11th and 12th graders only, but our 9th and 10th graders were given it as an additional subject.

In 2005, the center was moved to a room that is convenient for learning and has been providing various trainings to students. In the year 2012, the center got new 32 computers with better capacity and useful books for students, plasma videos and encyclopedia software containing more than 37400 articles. The computers was gained by the great effort of the then school principal father Tesfaye Kidane

Computer support was provided at affordable cost from non-governmental organization called CAMARA EDUCATION ETHIOPIA and included computers for Walame and Meskele Jesus 1st Primary Schools as well as the Ecote Center.

Since 2012, the center has added one more teacher and is providing various computer training to students.

OUR Services
  • Giving Formal ICT education through practical teaching for 9th to 12th grade students
  • Providing short trainings for teachers and management staff
  • Providing short computer training to school students in Dilla and surrounding areas
  • Giving "Coding for kids" training for selected 6-8 grade students from wallme and holly cross primary schools.
  • Providing information about our school through social media (Telegram, Facebook, YouTube)
  • To enable students to access thier textbooks, videos and various material by openning channels at their class level through Telegram
