ABOUT US

Bosco Coding & AI Club Background

Students participate in various co-curricular activities in their respective schools, depending on their interests and inclinations. The club not only serves as a platform to integrate classroom learning with practice, but also helps students learn teamwork, learn leadership skills, express themselves effectively, strengthen their connection with their environment, and expand their horizons.

Keeping this in mind, the Bosco Coding Club was established in our school in 2016 E.C. The club is doing various activities inside and outside the school. In terms of its established objective, it is making students have a special love and inclination for science and technology.

It has 9th to 12th grade students who are currently studying in our school and members who are studying and working in the country and abroad. The Club is working together with the members by coming up with different work plans every year.

ACTIVITIES

Bosco Coding & AI Club annual activities

Providing various computer trainning course to the members

In addition to the regular information communication Technology courses, the members of the club are given Selected computer training courses. Some of them are photoshop, web design, networking, programming.

Providing science and technology information Through mini media

Every Tuesday morning, in a good morning talk Ceremony, the members of the club present various Science and technology information to the teachers And students of our school. Here, we have made our School community have access to up-to-date Technological information.

Develop technology awareness programs for Other schools

In this program, the members of the club spread Awareness about technology to the students of 6th To 8th grade from our school. Among these schools, Holy cross, Wallame , New Generation and Michael Elementary Schools are some of them.

Preparing and selling gift cards for Christmas by members

Club Members prepare christmas gift cards by taking order from the students and we use the income of this program for different purposes for example we give the money to charity club and we also use the money to cover some of our expenses during tours.

Meeting with members every 15 days

Twice a month we discuss with the members about The work done in our club and through selected Students from each class they present new Technological discoveries to other members in PowerPoint presentation and video.

Visiting DIffernt technology institutes

The members of the club get more knowledge by visiting various technological institutes. Among the technology institutes visited by the members are Dila University, Southern Television Studio, Ethio Telecom Dila Branch, Dashan Bank Sesa Branch and Gedeo Zone Council Institute of Technology

Inviting different guests and making them share their Experiences

One of the Tasks of the club is to organize a program where Former students of our school who are working in Dilla City and the surrounding area can share their Educational and life experiences about the the technology institute where they work.

Preparing technology exhibitions in our School

Designating one day a year as Technology Day, It includes exhibiting the creative work done by Students on that day, providing fun and educational Games and information to our school community.
