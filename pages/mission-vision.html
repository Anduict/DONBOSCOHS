Our Vision statement

To be a school that is known for its commitment to excellence, its dedication to student success, and its unwavering focus on the future.

Our Mission statement

To prepare students to be successful in a rapidly changing world by providing a challenging and supportive educational experience.

our objective

  • The aim of the institute is to mainsteam the youth in the formal organized job market through market driven skill development interventions.
  • Facilitate skill training with the theory and practical along with human formation so as to enable them to enter industry as skilled craftsmen and thereby serve their country and fellow men as good and honest citizens.
  • Assist trainees in skill training to inculcate the spirit of enterpreneurship, quality consciousness, discipline, and good work habits that guarantee employment.
