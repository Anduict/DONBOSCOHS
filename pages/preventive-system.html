Preventive system of education

Don Bosco is known for his contributions to the field of education. He believed in a preventive system of education that focused on preventing problems before they occurred rather than reacting to them after the fact. This system is based on three principles: reason, religion, and kindness.

Don Bosco believed that education should be based on reason, meaning that it should be logical, practical, and relevant to the student's life. He believed that students should be taught how to think critically and solve problems on their own, rather than simply memorizing information.

Don Bosco was a devout Catholic and believed that education should be grounded in faith. He believed that students should be taught to love God and their neighbors, and that education should help students develop a sense of purpose and meaning in life.

Don Bosco believed that teachers should be kind and compassionate towards their students, treating them with respect and understanding. He believed that a positive relationship between teacher and student was essential for effective learning.
