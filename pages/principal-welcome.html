MESSAGE

message from the school principal

First of all, i would like to thank all of you who came to ask me about the school. I am the Director of Don Catholic Secondary School Dilla. A very warm wellcome to our school. When I look at you I don't see mere student I see the next generation of our country, and as a director of this school, I see myself as a guardian of our future. And it's a responsibility that I take very seriously, and I hope you would too.

My dear students, each and every one of you has the potential to become someone great in the world and help people if you put your mind to it, and work hard. I was told once that if every person can help one another in the world the this will be better place to live in. For this Education is the key to success, so keep up your good work, and work hard then you can accomplish anything you planned in your future.

Fainally, my message to my dear young people is that Life is not easy as we think so in orde to win this world we need to work hard. find yourself, struggle for life, today you may struggle but tommorow you will enjoy, today you may suffer but tommorow you will rest, today you may have a sleepless night but the day is coming with great hope, today you may sweat but tommorow you will enjoy the fruits. So yeasterday is history, today is life and tomorrow is mystery therfore never grow tired of doing what is right and good.

So today's pain and today's suffering is for tomorrow's success so therfore my dear student please learn something good form everyone you meet in life. work hard towards a better tomorrow.

Thank you !
Br. Asmaru Menegesha (SDB)

Head of school
